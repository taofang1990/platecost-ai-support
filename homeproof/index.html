HomeProof Organizer Support

Help for keeping purchase records, receipts, return dates, and warranty dates together on your iPhone.

Privacy Policy Privacy Choices Terms of Use

Using HomeProof

  • Add a purchase from Home or Items. You can enter its name, store, date, amount, notes, return deadline, and warranty end date.
  • Choose a receipt image or take a photo. The image is saved with that record on your device.
  • Enable return or warranty reminders on a record and allow notifications when iOS asks.
  • Search and filter records from Items. Open a record to edit or delete it.

Purchases and Restore

The first 20 records are available without an in-app purchase. HomeProof Pro offers a monthly auto-renewable subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase for unlimited records. Open Settings > Access to see available options or choose Restore Purchases. Apple manages billing and subscription cancellation in your Apple Account settings.

Purchase availability and displayed prices depend on the App Store storefront connected to your Apple Account.

家庭收据支持

帮助你在 iPhone 上整理购买记录、收据、退货日期和保修日期。

使用方法

  • 在“首页”或“条目”中添加购买记录，可填写商品、商店、日期、金额、备注、退货截止日期和保修日期。
  • 选择收据图片或拍照，图片会与记录一起保存在本机。
  • 开启退货或保修提醒，并在 iOS 提示时允许通知。
  • 在“条目”中搜索、筛选、查看、编辑或删除记录。

购买与恢复

前 20 条记录可免费使用。HomeProof Pro 提供月度自动续期订阅和一次性终身购买，均可解锁无限记录。进入“设置 > 使用权限”查看选项或选择“恢复购买”。账单和订阅取消由 Apple 账户设置管理。
