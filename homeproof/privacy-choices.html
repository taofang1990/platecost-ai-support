Privacy Choices

HomeProof Organizer · Effective date: November 1, 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

Your Records

HomeProof stores purchase records and receipt photos locally on your device. The app does not send these records to a HomeProof server or third-party AI provider. Open Items to edit or delete a record. Support cannot access or erase records remotely.

Photos and Reminders

Choose whether to import a photo or use the camera when adding a record. You can decline camera permission and continue using other features. You can enable or disable notification permission in iOS Settings; reminders are scheduled locally by iOS.

Purchases

Apple manages billing, subscription renewal, cancellation, and purchase history. You can manage a subscription in your Apple Account settings and use Restore Purchases from HomeProof Settings.

Questions

For privacy questions, email kaimen2026@163.com. This webpage cannot access, change, or delete information stored on your device. Please do not email receipt images or sensitive purchase details.

家庭收据 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

你的记录

家庭收据会在设备本地保存购买记录和收据照片，不会将这些记录发送到 HomeProof 服务器或第三方 AI 服务商。在“条目”中编辑或删除记录。支持人员无法远程查看或删除记录。

照片与提醒

添加记录时，你可以选择导入照片或使用相机。你可以拒绝相机权限并继续使用其他功能。可在 iOS“设置”中开启或关闭通知权限；提醒由 iOS 在本机安排。

购买

账单、订阅续期、取消和购买历史由 Apple 管理。你可以在 Apple 账户设置中管理订阅，并在家庭收据“设置”中恢复购买。

隐私问题

请发送邮件至 kaimen2026@163.com。本网页无法访问、修改或删除设备上保存的信息。请勿通过邮件发送收据图片或敏感购买信息。
