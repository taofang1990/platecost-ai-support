Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Overview

PlateCost AI is a menu cost and pricing app for restaurant operators. The current release candidate does not require account registration, does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not send recipe or cost inputs to PlateCost AI servers or an AI provider.

Information You Enter

You may enter recipe names, ingredients, cost assumptions, labor, packaging, waste, and margin targets. The app uses these inputs on your device to calculate estimates and rule-based pricing notes. Results are estimates based on your assumptions, not verified supplier quotes or current market prices.

Saved estimate history is stored in the app's local storage on your device. PlateCost AI does not receive this information. iOS device-backup behavior is governed by your device and Apple account settings.

Network and AI Processing

The current release candidate does not send recipe or cost inputs to DeepSeek, another AI provider, or a PlateCost AI server. Remote AI is not available in this version. If a future version adds remote processing, its in-app disclosure and this policy will be updated before that feature is enabled.

Purchases

Subscription purchases are not available in the current release candidate. If purchases are introduced in a future version, Apple will process them through your Apple ID.

Tracking

The current release candidate does not collect personal or recipe data on a PlateCost AI server, use advertising analytics, or track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
