Pocket Gratitude

Privacy Choices

Pocket Gratitude does not send journal entries to a remote AI provider or a Pocket Gratitude server in the current build. No remote-processing consent is collected because there is no remote AI request.

Manage data on your device

  • Turn off on-device AI in Settings. It is off by default and is available only when the system model is available.
  • Delete an individual entry from its detail screen.
  • Delete all entries from Settings.
  • Decline microphone and speech permissions; typing remains available.

This page cannot access or delete data stored on your device. Device-backup behavior follows iOS settings.
