Support

Help for your daily journal.

Entries and storage

Entries are stored in the app on your device. Device-backup behavior follows your iOS settings. Delete an entry from its detail screen or delete all entries in Settings.

Voice input

Voice is available only when on-device recognition is supported for your device and language. Typing remains available if recognition is unavailable or permission is declined.

AI reflections

On-device AI is optional and off by default. When available and enabled, Apple Foundation Models process your note on the device. Otherwise, a local template reflection is used.
