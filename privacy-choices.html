Privacy Choices

PlateCost AI does not create user accounts. Most recipe and pricing data stays on your device unless you run an AI estimate.

What You Can Control

  • Delete saved menu estimates from inside the app.
  • Reset the current draft before a new estimate.
  • Remove the app to clear all locally stored history from the device.

AI Requests

When you run AI analysis, the recipe and cost data you enter are sent only to generate the estimate. Do not include sensitive personal information in recipe fields.
