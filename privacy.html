Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 22, 2026

Overview

PlateCost AI is a menu cost and pricing assistant for restaurant operators. The app does not require account registration and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Information You Enter

You may enter recipe names, ingredients, cost assumptions, labor, packaging, waste, and margin targets. This information is used to calculate pricing estimates and generate AI-assisted suggestions.

Saved estimate history is stored locally on your device through the app's local storage.

AI Processing

When you run an AI estimate, relevant recipe and cost inputs may be sent to an AI service to generate pricing summaries, risk notes, and selling suggestions. Do not enter sensitive personal information into recipe fields.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment card information. Subscription management is handled through your Apple ID.

Tracking

PlateCost AI does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes.
