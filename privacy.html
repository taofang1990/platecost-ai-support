Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Overview

PlateCost AI is a menu cost and pricing app for restaurant operators. It does not require account registration or use cross-app tracking. Core cost and price calculations run on your device. Optional remote AI pricing suggestions require your consent before the first request.

Information You Enter

You may enter recipe names, ingredients, quantities, costs, labor, packaging, waste, margin targets, and a market. The app uses these inputs on your device to calculate costs and price estimates. Results are based on your assumptions, not verified supplier quotes or current market prices.

Saved estimate history is stored in the app's local storage on your device. PlateCost AI does not receive this information. iOS device-backup behavior is governed by your device and Apple account settings.

Network and AI Processing

When you consent to remote AI analysis, the app sends the dish name, serving count, ingredient names, quantities, units and unit costs, selected market, and calculated cost and price figures over HTTPS to the PlateCost AI relay for processing by DeepSeek. This is used only to generate written pricing suggestions; numerical calculations remain on your device.

The configured relay and model-provider route processes requests in real time. It does not retain request contents or related logs, and does not use inputs or outputs to improve models. If you decline consent, that analysis request is not sent and the app uses on-device rule-based suggestions. You can withdraw consent in Settings; future requests will not be sent unless you consent again.

AI suggestions may be inaccurate or incomplete. Use them as a reference and verify supplier prices before making business decisions. Do not enter personal or confidential information.

Purchases

Premium offers optional monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions for unlimited remote AI estimates. Apple processes purchases through your Apple ID; PlateCost AI does not receive your payment-card details. The app shows the price and renewal terms before purchase. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

Tracking

PlateCost AI does not use advertising analytics or track you across other companies' apps or websites. Recipe and pricing requests are processed transiently and are not retained by the configured relay/provider route.
