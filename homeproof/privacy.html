Privacy Policy

HomeProof Organizer · Effective date: November 1, 2026

SupportPrivacy Choices

Overview

HomeProof Organizer helps you keep purchase records and receipt photos on your device. The app does not require an account and does not operate a HomeProof server, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or AI-processing service. The app does not send your purchase records, notes, or receipt images to HomeProof or an AI provider.

Information Stored on Your Device

If you use the app, you may enter an item name, merchant, purchase date, amount and currency, category, notes, return deadline, warranty expiration date, and reminder preferences. You may also import or take a receipt photo. These records and images are stored in HomeProof's local app storage so the app can display and manage them.

HomeProof asks iOS to exclude its saved record storage and receipt images from device backups. Backup behavior is controlled by Apple and the operating system. Because HomeProof has no server copy, deleting the app, losing the device, or losing its local storage may permanently remove records that you have not kept elsewhere.

Photos, Camera, and Notifications

A photo you choose is copied into the app's local storage. Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo. HomeProof does not request broad access to your photo library.

If you enable reminders and grant notification permission, iOS schedules local notifications on your device. Notification previews may show an item name and return or warranty date context. You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes the transaction and payment information under Apple's terms and privacy practices. HomeProof receives transaction and entitlement results needed to unlock the purchased app features; HomeProof does not receive your payment card credentials. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing, Retention, and Deletion

HomeProof does not transmit the locally stored records or receipt photos to us or to third-party AI, advertising, or analytics providers. The information remains on your device until you delete a record or remove the app. You can edit or delete records in Items. Support cannot access or remotely erase local app data.

Support Website Hosting

This policy and the HomeProof support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy practices. HomeProof does not receive or control those hosting logs. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children

HomeProof is a general-purpose household organizer and does not ask users to provide personal information to an online service. Records entered into the app remain in local app storage on the device.

Changes and Contact

We will update this page if the app's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact kaimen2026@163.com. Please do not send receipt images or sensitive purchase details by email.

隐私政策

家庭收据 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

概述

家庭收据帮助你在设备上保存购买记录和收据照片。App 无需账号，不提供 HomeProof 服务器、云同步、广告、分析或 AI 处理服务。App 不会将购买记录、备注或收据图片发送给 HomeProof 或 AI 服务商。

保存在设备上的信息

你可以输入商品名称、商家、购买日期、金额和币种、分类、备注、退货截止日期、保修到期日期及提醒偏好，也可以导入或拍摄收据照片。这些资料和图片保存在 App 的本地存储中，供 App 展示和管理。

HomeProof 会请求 iOS 将记录数据和收据图片排除在设备备份之外。备份行为由 Apple 和操作系统控制。HomeProof 没有服务器副本；如果删除 App、丢失设备或本地存储，且没有自行保留其他副本，记录可能永久丢失。

照片、相机和通知

你选择的照片会复制到 App 的本地存储。只有在你选择拍照时才会请求相机权限。HomeProof 不请求整个照片图库的广泛访问权限。

开启提醒并授予通知权限后，iOS 会在本机安排通知。通知预览可能显示商品名称和退货或保修日期。你可以在 iOS“设置”中管理通知权限。

购买

App 内购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。Apple 按其条款和隐私实践处理交易及付款信息。HomeProof 会接收解锁功能所需的交易和权益结果，但不会接收银行卡凭据。详情请阅读 Apple 隐私政策。

共享、保存期限与删除

HomeProof 不会将本地记录或收据照片传输给我们，也不会传输给第三方 AI、广告或分析服务商。信息会保留在你的设备上，直到你删除记录或移除 App。你可以在“条目”中编辑或删除记录。支持人员无法访问或远程删除本地数据。

支持网站托管

本隐私政策和家庭收据支持页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 会根据自身隐私实践出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。HomeProof 不会接收或控制这些托管日志。请参阅 GitHub Pages 说明和 GitHub 隐私声明。

儿童

家庭收据是一款通用家庭整理工具，不要求用户向在线服务提供个人信息。输入的记录保存在设备本地。

政策更新与联系

如果 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面。如有隐私问题，请联系 kaimen2026@163.com。请勿通过邮件发送收据图片或敏感购买信息。
