Pocket Gratitude

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026.

Journal information

Journal notes, dates, moods, reflections, and artwork seeds are stored in the app's data on your device. The app does not upload these entries to a Pocket Gratitude server.

On-device processing

On-device AI is off by default. If enabled on a compatible device, Apple Foundation Models use the journal note and optional mood hint on that device to generate a reflection and mood label. The app does not send this content to an external AI service. Local template reflections remain available when on-device AI is off or unavailable.

Voice input

The app requests microphone and speech-recognition access only after you tap Voice. Recording starts only when on-device recognition is available for the device and language. Audio is processed on the device and the app does not save recordings. Typing remains available when offline recognition is unavailable.

Storage and deletion

Entries remain on the device until you delete an individual entry or all entries in the app. Device-backup behavior follows iOS settings. Deleting an in-app entry does not directly modify historical copies in an Apple device backup.

Sharing and tracking

This version does not require an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or make app-initiated network requests. Journal content is not sold, used for advertising tracking, or sent to a third-party AI service.

Your choices

You can turn off on-device AI in Settings and delete individual entries or all entries. When on-device AI is off, new reflections use local templates.
